Our Story

More than coffee —
a place to belong.

The Daily Grind was born from a simple idea: everyone deserves a warm corner of the world to call their own. Since 2014, we've been roasting our beans in-house, baking everything fresh every morning, and building a community one cup at a time.

We source our single-origin beans directly from small farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — because great coffee starts long before it reaches your cup. Pull up a chair, stay as long as you like, and let us take care of the rest.
